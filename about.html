About Me

Currently I’m an undergraduate Psychology student in the Faculty of Science at the University of Waterloo (Ontario, Canada). Often there are terms I’m not taking any psychology course that’s actually about psychology. So to me, I am no more empathetic than a student in any other major. Sometime during my 2nd year I decided to minor in Neuroscience and I would highly recommend it because it got me to take more kinesiology and biology courses that I wouldn't have otherwise.

I love my major but I’m also interested in coding websites and software + creative work like story writing and drawing. To me, it’s important for everyone to try new things and read hella books so we each build our own unique libraries of knowledge.

About This

Let’s start with what this isn’t. This isn’t a resume. This isn’t monk level wisdom for how to beat university. And this isn’t a blog (debatable).

It’s more like a manifestation of my fear of dementia. I want to know I offloaded as much of my University experience as I could somewhere so there’s no chance of forgetting it. Most of the content is focused on what I learned near the end of my undergraduate. This is not by design, this is by my poor memory. But from a higher level, like maybe up on a horse, I started creating all this with the vision of helping psychology science undergraduates navigate their courses and most of all recent graduates or seniors that think they learned nothing. You did not learn nothing! And you will not forget everything the second you graduate. So, that’s what this is about.
