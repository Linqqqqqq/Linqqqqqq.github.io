About This

Let’s start with what this isn’t. This isn’t a résumé. This isn’t a list of dos & don'ts for how to beat university. And this isn’t a blog (debatable).

It’s more like a manifestation of my fear of dementia. I want to know I offloaded as much of my university experience as I could somewhere so there’s no chance of forgetting it. Most of the content is focused on what I learned near the end of my undergraduate. This is not by design, this is by my poor memory. But from a higher level, like maybe up on a horse, I started creating all this with the vision of helping psychology undergraduates navigate their courses and most of all recent graduates or seniors that are worried about their future. You did not learn nothing! And you will not forget everything the second you graduate. That’s what this is about.
